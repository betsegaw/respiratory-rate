Breathing Rate Monitor

Continuous ambient estimate from phone motion sensors. How it works →

Not a medical device. Not an apnea monitor. This is an experimental estimate for curiosity only — it can be wrong or miss events entirely. If breathing stops, the app will NOT sound a red alarm — a stopped or very weak signal shows as "Signal uncertain," not an alert. Never rely on it to watch a sleeping child. A quiet screen does not mean a child is safe. If you are worried, look, count manually, and contact a clinician.
Also beep on alarm

How to use: Lay the phone flat and gently on the child's chest or belly (screen up) so it rises and falls with each breath. Tap start and keep it still. The estimate updates every ~10 seconds on a rolling 30-second window. Typical toddler resting rate is ~20–40 bpm.
